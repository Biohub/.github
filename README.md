Biohub is building the first large-scale scientific initiative combining frontier AI with frontier biology to accelerate discoveries and advance our mission. We build technology and provide it openly to help scientists around the world use AI-powered biology to study how cells operate, organize, and work as part of systems to understand why disease happens and how to correct it.

In 2025, EvolutionaryScale, a frontier AI research lab behind groundbreaking life sciences AI, joined Biohub to accelerate this mission. The ESM models and resources you know and trust are now open and available through Biohub’s open platform, united under a broader mission to power your next breakthrough. Learn more about Biohub [here](https://biohub.org/).

## Code of Conduct
